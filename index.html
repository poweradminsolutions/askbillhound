Straight answers

The questions you should be asking.

Is the analysis really free?

Yes, with no obligation attached. He analyzes the bill, shows you what stands out and the realistic range, and you can walk away with it. The free analysis is the front door, not a trap. He'd rather be the guy who told you the truth about your bill than the guy who held it hostage.

Who sees my bill?

Almost nobody, and that's by design. Your bill travels over a secure connection and gets analyzed right here in the U.S. It's not emailed around an office or shipped to an offshore processing center the way some bill services do it. No prying eyes, no reselling, no marketing lists. The account number on it is useless to us and we treat it that way. And if you want it deleted after your analysis, just ask, text or email, and it's done.

What's the catch on the free analysis?

Some people take the findings and make the call themselves. Some look at 45 minutes of hold music and hand it to him. Both happen every day, and both are fine. The free analysis is how you find out which one you are.

If I hire him, how can he negotiate for me?

You sign a one-page authorization that lets him deal with your provider on your behalf. Same thing a lawyer or a family member would use. You e-sign it from your phone in about a minute, and it only covers the bill you sent.

Do I have to get on the phone?

Not if you hire him. That's the point of him. He handles the hold music, the retention department, the "let me transfer you." You get a text when it's done.

Will my service change?

Not unless you say yes first. His first move is always the same service at a lower price. If a better deal means a change, he brings it to you and you decide. Nothing moves without your go-ahead.

What if he finds nothing?

Then the analysis says so plainly, you've spent nothing, and you know your bill is clean. That answer has value too. If you hired him and the negotiation wins nothing, you pay nothing.
