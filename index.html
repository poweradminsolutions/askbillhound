Straight answers

The questions you should be asking.

Is this legit?

Fair question. Bill negotiation is a real industry, and the honest version of it works exactly one way: no money up front, paid only from verified savings. That's how Billhound runs. You see every number before anything happens, and you approve the fight before it starts.

How can he negotiate for me?

You sign a one-page authorization that lets him deal with your provider on your behalf. Same thing a lawyer or a family member would use. You e-sign it from your phone in about a minute, and it only covers the bill you sent.

Do I have to get on the phone?

No. That's the point of him. He handles the hold music, the retention department, the "let me transfer you." You get a text when it's done.

Will my service change?

Not unless you say yes first. His first move is always the same service at a lower price. If a better deal means a change, he brings it to you and you decide. Nothing moves without your go-ahead.

What if he finds nothing?

Then you pay nothing, and he tells you straight: your bill is clean. No fee, no follow-up sales pitch, no "well, actually." The only way he makes a dollar is by putting more than that dollar back in your pocket first.
