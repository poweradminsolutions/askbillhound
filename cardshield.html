Quick answers

Why is it free for me?

Card Shield and Billhound are affiliated companies, and Card Shield includes his service as a member benefit. Your membership covers the fee he'd normally take from the savings. There's no catch to find, which is the point of putting it in writing.

Do I still sign something?

Yes, one short authorization per bill. It lets him talk to that one provider about that one account, nothing broader, and it expires when the negotiation ends. You e-sign it from your phone in under a minute.

Does this touch my Card Shield program?

No. Billhound is a separate service, and nothing about your Card Shield program, account, or standing changes. He just lowers your bills.
